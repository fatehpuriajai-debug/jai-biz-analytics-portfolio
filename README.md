# Jai Biz Analytics Portfolio

Build a clean, modern, mobile-responsive personal portfolio website for Jai Fatehpuria, a first-year BBA student at JECRC University, Jaipur. Sections: Home, About, Skills, Projects, Education, Contact, with a sticky navigation bar. Home: his name, a short introduction, his interests in Business Analytics and Marketing, and “View Projects” and “Contact” buttons. About: education, interests, and the career goal of pursuing an MBA from a reputed institution. Skills: [only skills I actually have]. Projects: two projects with name, description and technologies used. Education: BBA, JECRC University, 2025–2028. Contact: email, LinkedIn and GitHub links only. Use a professional blue and white theme. Do not invent any personal information, projects, certificates or achievements.
